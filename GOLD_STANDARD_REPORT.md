## Phase 2 — QR Code Generator (pure Solana tx, no WalletConnect)

- [app/lib/tx-qr.ts](app/lib/tx-qr.ts) — reads the Anchor 0.31 IDL, borsh-encodes any instruction's args
  (discriminator from IDL), **auto-derives PDAs from IDL seed specs** (const / account / arg seeds),
  builds an unsigned `Transaction`, serializes it, base58-encodes it, and emits `solana:<base58Tx>`.
- [app/components/TransactionQRBuilder.tsx](app/components/TransactionQRBuilder.tsx) — admin picks **any
  instruction** from the IDL, fills args/accounts, gets a scannable QR (SVG, `qrcode.react`).
- IDL copied to [app/lib/idl/my_project.json](app/lib/idl/my_project.json).
- No relay, no WalletConnect — payload is the raw pre-built transaction.

## Phase 3 — Admin Transfer & Airdrop

- Program: added `transfer_treasury(amount)` and `airdrop(amount)` — admin-only, funds move from the

- UI: [app/components/TreasuryActionsPanel.tsx](app/components/TreasuryActionsPanel.tsx) generates the
